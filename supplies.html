About Us

A supply partner for food service operators

All-Serve Supply provides wholesale disposables to restaurants, food trucks, cafés and caterers. We stock the everyday consumables a kitchen moves through in volume — containers, bags, cups, lids, bottles and gloves — and price them by the case so ordering in bulk actually costs less per unit.

We keep the range deliberately focused. Rather than list thousands of items, we carry the sizes and formats that working kitchens reorder week after week, which keeps stock consistent and lead times short.

Ordering is straightforward. Send us a request with the items and quantities you're after and we'll come back with current case pricing and availability. Nothing is charged or shipped until we've confirmed the details with you directly — there's no account to set up and no minimum to get a quote.

How ordering works

  1. Send a request

    List the items and quantities you need. No account, no minimum.

  2. We confirm the details

    We come back with current case pricing, pack counts and availability.

  3. You approve, then we ship

    Nothing is charged or dispatched until you've signed off on the quote.
